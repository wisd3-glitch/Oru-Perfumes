1. Who we are

Oru Perfumes ("Oru", "we", "us") is an online fragrance shop. The business that decides how your personal data is used (the data controller) is [registered business name], [registered address], [contact email]. This policy explains how we handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and other applicable Nigerian law.

2. Information we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and any order notes.
  • Order information: what you ordered, the delivery option, the order total and the payment method chosen.
  • Messages: anything you send through our contact form, plus your newsletter sign-up if you subscribe.
  • Technical data (live shop): device and browser information and pages visited, if we use analytics. [Confirm and list any analytics tools used.]

We do not store your card details. Card payments are handled by the payment provider.

3. How we use your information

  • To process, deliver and support your orders.
  • To contact you about an order or an enquiry.
  • To send our newsletter, only if you have agreed.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

4. Our lawful basis

The NDPA requires a lawful basis for using personal data. We rely on: contract (to fulfil the order you place), consent (for marketing emails, which you can withdraw at any time), legal obligation (for example tax and accounting records) and, where appropriate, legitimate interests (such as preventing fraud). [Confirm the bases that apply to your actual processing.]

5. Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with: delivery partners (name, phone and address), our payment provider, and IT or email service providers who work on our behalf. We do not sell your personal data. If data is transferred outside Nigeria, we will make sure the transfer meets the requirements of the NDPA. [List the actual providers.]

6. How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed for delivery, support and legal or accounting requirements: [retention period, for example 6 years]. Marketing details are kept until you unsubscribe. Enquiries are kept for [period].

7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you have rights over your personal data, including to be informed about how it is used, to access it, to have inaccurate data corrected, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to receive your data in a portable format. To use any of these rights, contact us at [privacy contact email]. If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

8. Security and breaches

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify the people and authorities concerned as the law requires.

9. Children

Oru is not directed at children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place orders and contact us on your behalf.

10. Browser storage in this demo

This case-study website stores your bag, saved fragrances, latest demo order and any demo account details (name, email, phone and address, but never a password) in your own browser (local storage) so the pages can work together. This data stays on your device, is not sent to Oru, and can be cleared in your browser settings. The demo does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. For privacy questions, contact [data protection contact name] at [privacy contact email].
