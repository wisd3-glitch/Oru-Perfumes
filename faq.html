General

01. How do I choose the right fragrance?

Start with the family you usually enjoy (fresh, floral, woody or amber), then read the top, heart and base notes on each product page. You can also filter and search the shop, or contact us with a description of what you like.

02. What is the difference between Extrait, Eau de Parfum and Eau de Toilette?

They differ mainly in concentration. Extrait is the most concentrated and usually lasts longest, Eau de Parfum sits in the middle, and Eau de Toilette is lighter. Each product page shows its concentration.

03. How long does a fragrance last?

It depends on the concentration, the notes and your skin. Many people find a stronger concentration lasts longer. Applying to clean, moisturised skin helps.

04. Are your fragrances authentic?

Authenticity is the point of Oru. In a live shop this answer would explain exactly where bottles are sourced and how they are checked. The products in this case study are fictional samples.

Orders & delivery

01. How long does delivery take?

Sample estimates: standard delivery takes 3 to 7 working days and express delivery 1 to 3 working days. The estimate is shown at checkout.

02. Is delivery free?

Standard delivery is free on orders above ₦250,000. Below that it costs ₦5,000, and express delivery costs ₦12,000. These are sample rates.

03. Can I track my order?

A live shop would send an order confirmation with your order number and delivery updates. This demo does not send any messages.

04. Can I change my address after ordering?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order number. We can usually update an address before the order has been dispatched.

Returns & support

01. Can I return a fragrance?

Sealed, unopened bottles can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Opened fragrance cannot be returned for hygiene reasons. See Delivery & returns for details. This is a sample policy.

02. What if my order arrives damaged?

Contact us within 48 hours with your order number and photos of the damage, and we will arrange a replacement or refund. Sample policy.

03. How do I contact Oru?

Use the contact page. In this demo the contact details are placeholders.

Payment & security

01. Which payment methods do you accept?

The checkout shows card payment, bank transfer and pay on delivery as placeholders. A live shop would connect a payment provider such as Paystack.

02. Is my payment information stored?

This demo takes no payment and stores no card details. In a live shop, card payments would be handled by the payment provider.

You can enter a code in your bag. The demo code is DEMO10, which takes 10% off.
