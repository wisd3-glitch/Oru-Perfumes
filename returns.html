3. Processing time

Orders are checked and packed within [1 to 2] working days. We will contact you if an item is unavailable.

4. If delivery fails

If a delivery cannot be completed because of an incorrect address or no one being available, the courier may contact you to arrange a new attempt. Additional delivery charges may apply. [Confirm with your courier.]

5. Returns

You can return a fragrance if all of the following apply:

  • it is sealed, unopened and unused;
  • you contact us within 7 days of delivery;
  • it is returned in its original packaging with proof of purchase.

For hygiene and safety, opened fragrance cannot be returned unless it is faulty.

6. Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos. We will arrange a replacement or refund.

7. How to return an item

Contact us with your order number and the reason for the return. We will confirm the return address and steps. Return delivery is paid by the customer unless the item is damaged, faulty or incorrect. [Confirm.]

8. Refunds

Once we receive and check a returned item, we will refund it to the original payment method within [7 to 10] working days. Delivery fees are refunded only where the return is due to our error.
